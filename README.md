# 🚤 River Waste Collection Boat

## 🌊 Project Overview

The River Waste Collection Boat is a prototype designed to collect floating waste from rivers, lakes, and other water bodies.

The system uses a battery-powered propulsion system and a mechanical waste-collection mechanism to gather floating plastic and other waste from the water surface.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Floating plastic and other waste pollute rivers and water bodies, affecting aquatic ecosystems and reducing water quality.

Manual waste collection can be difficult, time-consuming, and unsafe.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Our solution is a small, low-cost waste-collecting boat that can move across the water and collect floating waste.

### Main Features

- 🚤 Battery-powered boat
- ⚙️ DC motor propulsion
- 🔋 Rechargeable battery
- 🛞 Propeller-based movement
- ♻️ Mechanical waste collection mechanism
- 🌊 Designed for floating waste
- 💰 Low-cost prototype

## ⚙️ Working Principle

1. The battery supplies power to the DC motors.
2. The motors rotate the propellers.
3. The boat moves across the water.
4. The collection mechanism captures floating waste.
5. Collected waste is stored for later disposal.

## 🧰 Components Used

- DC Motors
- Battery
- Propellers
- Motor/Power connections
- Boat body
- Mechanical waste collection mechanism

## 🌱 Environmental Impact

The project aims to support cleaner water bodies by helping remove floating waste and reducing pollution.
